The columns in the dataset have the following meanings:

player: The player's name.
tour: ATP or WTA.
country: The country the player represents. If this is 'World', avoid making references to the player's nationality.
ranking: The player's ranking as of September 28th, 2026.

These fields represent playing statistics that overall encompass playing style.
aces: Number of aces per first serve that goes in.
first_in: First serve percentage.
first_won: Percentage of points won on first serve.
second_in: Second serve percentage.
second_won: Percentage of points won on second serve.
first_ret_won:  Percentage of points won on first serve return.
second_ret_won:  Percentage of points won on second serve return.
winners: Percentage of winners to overall points played.
unforced: Percentage of unforced to overall points played, subtracted from 1. This means a high score correlates to a low error frequency.
net: Percentage of points the player approached the net to overall points played.
net_won: Percentage of points won after approaching the net.
hard: Win rate on hard courts.
clay: Win rate on clay courts.
grass: Win rate on grass courts.
strong_stat: The player's strongest stat.
weak_stat: The player's weakest stat.
strong_surface: The player's strongest surface.
weak_surface: The player's weakest surface.
stat_offset: A measure of the difference between this player's stats and the user's favourites.
strong_diff: A measure of stat-offset for this player's strongest stat.
weak_diff: A measure of stat-offset for this player's weakest stat.

In the other csv file:
value: Statistical significance. Only talk about the top 2-4 stats in this column.
scaled_mean: A measure of how good the user's favourite players are on this stat. If this is negative, the players are generally poor at this.
